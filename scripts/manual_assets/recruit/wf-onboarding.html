Convert to Employee One action on an accepted offer automatically creates the employee record, starts preboarding at the first enabled stage, and generates the onboarding checklist below — then notifies HR, IT, Admin and Finance.
PHASE 1

Pre-Joining (before Day 1)

due dates relative to joining date
  • Paperwork pack — HR Docs, due −3d
  • NDA & policy acknowledgments — HR Docs, due Day 0
  • System access & email — IT Setup, due −1d
  • Laptop / workstation — IT Setup, due −1d
PHASE 2

Day 1 & First Week

first-week integration
  • Payroll setup & benefits — Payroll, due +1d
  • Team introductions — Introduction, due Day 0
  • Buddy check-in — Buddy Setup, due +1d
PHASE 3

Ramp-up (30 days)

productivity & goals
  • Training modules & KRAs — Training, due +7d
  • Project / team allocation review — Allocation, due +14d
Template precedence: if Onboarding Task Templates are configured and "generate from templates" is enabled, templates win over this checklist; otherwise these configurable phases/tasks are used.

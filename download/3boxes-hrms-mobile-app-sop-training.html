Chapter 1
About This Guide & Installing the App

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and training guide describes how to use the 3Boxes HRMS mobile application in day-to-day work. It is written for two audiences at once: employees, who use the app to punch attendance, apply for leave, check payslips, work on project tasks, respond in chat and keep their own profile up to date; and HR / Admin users (including the CEO and managers with admin rights), who additionally manage employee records, approve requests, review reports and monitor team attendance from the same app. Everything in this guide reflects app version v1.7.0 (build 34) — if your app shows an older version on the login or profile screen, please update first using the steps below. Version 1.7.0 ships the redesigned login experience, the HR Help Desk, the announcements banner, dual-channel OTP delivery (SMS + email), a Contact HR ticket form right on the login screen, and a compact one-screen Home dashboard with a colourful Quick Access grid.

Download policy: the 3Boxes HRMS mobile app is distributed as an Android APK file, downloadable only from the web login page of your company workspace. There is no Play Store listing and no PWA/website shortcut — installing from anywhere else is not supported.

Install the app (one time)

Open your company workspace in the phone browser — for example marqaitechgroup.3boxeshrms.com — or simply open 3boxeshrms.com. Do this before logging in; the download section sits on the login page itself.
Tap the Android app download button in the login page's download section. The current release APK (v1.7.0) downloads to your device.
Allow the install. Android asks for permission to install from your browser ("Install unknown apps"). Grant it once, then confirm the installation prompt.
Upgrades are in-place. The app is signed with a permanent release key, so every new version installs directly over the old one — no uninstall needed. When HR announces an update, repeat steps 1–3 to fetch the latest APK.
First launch

On first launch the app shows the splash screen and asks you to accept the terms of use. Accept them once and you move straight to the sign-in screen described in Chapter 2. If your company uses a dedicated workspace link, prefer opening that tenant link in the browser for the download — it guarantees you get the build configured for your organisation.

Chapter 2
Getting Started — Login & Workspace

Version 1.7.0 keeps the redesigned sign-in experience and polishes every rough edge reported by the field: the Employee ID tab always opens the full alphanumeric keyboard (letters and numbers, no more stuck numeric pad), Remember me now works on both tabs and pre-fills your details on the next launch, the one-time code reaches you by SMS and email, and Contact HR raises a real help-desk ticket that instantly alerts HR / Admin / IT Admin by app notification and email — all before you have managed to sign in. The app connects to the same accounts as the web application, and the workspace detection ladder (demo workspace → central hub → your company's dedicated workspace) still runs automatically and transparently underneath.

The sign-in flow, step by step

Welcome screen. The app opens on a deep-navy starry sky with the 3Boxes HRMS logo card and the tagline "Your workplace, in one place". Tap the arrow button to continue to sign-in.
Choose a sign-in method. The sign-in screen has a violet gradient header with the logo and two tabs: Mobile Number and Employee ID. Pick whichever is easier for you — both lead to the same account.
Mobile Number tab: select your country code (+91 by default), enter your registered mobile number, optionally leave Remember me on so the number is pre-filled next time, and tap Continue →. The app sends a 6-digit one-time code by SMS to your phone and by email to the registered address, then opens the verification screen.
Employee ID tab: tap the tab and the full alphanumeric keyboard opens immediately — type your Employee ID or work email plus your password, keep Remember me on to auto-save your identity on this device, and tap Sign In. Forgot password? sits right next to it — tap it and contact your HR team for a reset, exactly as on web.
Verify your number (OTP). The verification screen shows the masked destinations, six code boxes, a resend countdown and a Change number link. A status chip under the headline tells you exactly which channel carried the code — SMS, email or both. Enter the code and tap Verify & continue.
Enable fingerprint / Face ID. After the first successful sign-in the app offers biometric unlock (the same Face ID / Fingerprint row is visible on both sign-in tabs). Enable it on personal phones — the next launch opens with a fingerprint prompt instead of the keyboard.
OTP tips — dual-channel delivery

The code now goes out on two channels at once: SMS to your registered mobile number and email to your registered inbox (check spam). The chip on the verification screen reports what actually went out, so you never wait on a message that was never sent. The code is single-use and expires quickly; requesting a new one invalidates the old. If no channel could deliver — for example before your SMS gateway is configured — the screen says so immediately and offers a one-tap Raise a ticket to HR shortcut; on the demo workspace it also shows the code inline so the flow stays testable.

Contact HR — raises a real ticket

Both sign-in tabs end with a Contact HR link, and it is no longer just an information sheet: tapping it opens a short form (issue type — OTP not received, sign-in problem, account locked, password reset, unknown Employee ID — plus optional Employee ID, name and reply-to contact). Submitting creates a real help-desk ticket in your workspace's Support & AI → Help Desk queue, and every HR / Admin / IT Admin receives an in-app alert and an email (plus an SMS nudge when a gateway is configured) within seconds. You get the ticket number on screen — no colleague login needed.

Security basics: never share your login or the one-time codes; biometric unlock is tied to the device's own fingerprint/face store, so a colleague's finger will simply not open your app. On shared devices skip biometric setup and sign out after use.

Chapter 4
HR Help Desk — Support Ticketing on the Go

The HR Help Desk (Quick Links tile, headset icon) is the mobile window into the same support ticketing system the web app uses under Support & AI → Help Desk. A ticket raised on the phone appears instantly in the web queue for HR/Admin, replies flow both ways, and status changes sync in real time — one ticketing system, two front-ends, zero duplication.

Working a ticket from the phone

Raise a ticket. Open the Help Desk tile and tap the create button. Pick a category, write a clear subject and description, and submit — HR/Admin are notified immediately. Cannot sign in at all? The login screen's Contact HR form raises the same ticket without any session, asking for your Employee ID and issue — HR / Admin / IT Admin get the alert by app notification and email.
Track "My tickets". Employees see their own tickets with live status (open / in progress / resolved / closed) — the server enforces the self-scope, so nobody can read a colleague's tickets.
Reply in the thread. Open a ticket to read the full conversation and add comments; every reply is visible to HR/Admin on web the moment it lands.
HR/Admin operations view. HR/Admin additionally see all tenant tickets, answer threads from the phone and change ticket status as they work through the queue — useful between meetings without opening a laptop.
Good ticket hygiene: one issue per ticket, a descriptive subject ("Payslip September — HRA component wrong" beats "help"), and attach context up front. Clear tickets get resolved faster and the history stays searchable for both sides.
Chapter 5
Attendance SOP — Punch, Break, Regularise

Attendance on mobile uses the same punch pipeline as the biometric and web channels: every Check In / Check Out runs through the shared PunchFlow, which captures your GPS position and validates it before recording the punch. This protects the accuracy of attendance data — punches taken from impossible locations or with spoofed GPS are rejected. Punches recorded on mobile flow straight into the same attendance records your HR team reviews on the web, so there is nothing extra to submit or reconcile.

Daily punching procedure

Enable location services before punching — the app needs GPS to stamp the punch with a valid position. Keep the device's time automatic.
Check In when your shift starts — from the Home quick card or the Attendance module. The card shows today's status, working hours so far and your latest punch time.
Start Break when you step away and End Break when you return. Break windows are recorded against the day and reflected in your worked-hours total.
Check Out at the end of the shift. The punch card flips back and your day is complete — in time, out time, breaks and total hours.
Forgot to punch?

Do not panic and do not double-punch. Raise a regularisation request from the Attendance module (or the Raise shortcut) stating the correct in/out times. Your manager and HR receive it and, once approved, the day's attendance is corrected at the source — payroll then reads the corrected data.

If GPS fails

If the punch returns a location error: confirm Location is ON for the app in Android settings, move near a window or open space, and retry. The app blocks mock-location providers — removing any fake-GPS app resolves persistent rejections.

For HR / Admin — Team Attendance

The Team Attendance tile (admin, CEO and HR only) shows who is present, on leave or missing punches for the day, with a daily status entry view. Combined with the Reports module (attendance and leave reports), it replaces the old "ask around" morning routine — open it first thing and you have the whole organisation's attendance posture on one screen.

Chapter 6
Leave SOP — Apply, Track, Approve

Leave requests raised on mobile go through exactly the same workflow as on web: the request is created against your leave policy, your approver chain is resolved automatically, and every decision triggers a notification (and email) to the people involved. Balances shown in the app are the live balances HR maintains, so what you see is what payroll will honour.

Apply for leave

Open Leave Management (or tap Request Leave on Home) and tap Apply Leave.
Choose the leave type — casual, sick, earned and any company-specific types configured by HR.
Pick the dates and enter a short reason. Apply before the leave date wherever possible — planned leave applied in advance is approved faster and protects payroll.
Submit. The request appears in your list with a status chip: Pending, Approved or Rejected. You are notified the moment a decision is made.
Track balances

The Leave home shows your remaining balance per leave type alongside this year's usage. Check it before applying — the form warns when a request exceeds the balance, but the balance view is the reliable place to plan long breaks.

For approvers — act on the go

HR/Admin, CEO and managers with approval rights see Approve and Reject actions directly on each pending card, and can apply leave on behalf of an employee from the same screen (useful for field staff who cannot raise it themselves). Every action is audit-logged.

Chapter 7
Payroll SOP — Payslips on the Go

The Payroll module brings your payslip history to the phone. Payslips appear here once payroll for the month is processed and released by the payroll manager — the mobile list mirrors the web payroll records exactly, including the official company payslip format used by the organisation. Employees do not need to request payslips from HR any more; the current and historical months are self-service.

View a payslip

Open Payroll from the Modules grid. The Payslips list shows each processed month with its net salary.
Filter if needed — by month, by employee (for HR/Admin and payroll managers reviewing a specific person) or by status, using the filter bar.
Tap a payslip to open the detail sheet: earnings, deductions and the net pay breakdown for that month. Verify the figures and raise a ticket if something looks wrong — do not wait for payday.
Note for HR / payroll managers: mobile payroll respects the web's release rules — payslip details follow the company payroll policy, and actions such as downloading released payslips are governed by the payroll-manager permission set configured on web. If a payslip you expect is missing, check that the payroll run for that month has been released.
Chapter 8
Projects, Tasks & Collaboration

Project work no longer requires a laptop. The Projects module mirrors the web project workspace: you see the projects you are allocated to, the tasks assigned to you and their live status. When a task is created for you or a task moves to a new workflow step, you receive a push notification on the phone and an email — the same dual-channel alert that web users get — so nothing sits unnoticed until the next stand-up.

  • Projects — open the tile to see your allocated projects; tap one for its detail view with tasks, members and progress.
  • Tasks — check what is assigned to you, review descriptions, dates and attachments posted from web or mobile, and move your work forward by updating the task as you go.
  • Chat — Chat lives in the bottom navigation (and behind the floating bubble on Home and the web dashboard), now wearing a SmartHR-style redesign: a royal-blue gradient header, counter chips for All · Direct · Channels · Unread, a Quick chat strip of recent one-to-one partners with online dots, and red unread badges. Open company and project-specific conversations, see member lists, and coordinate there instead of personal messaging apps, so decisions stay inside the organisation's record. The same conversations are reachable from the chat icon on the web dashboard — start a thread on the phone, continue it on the laptop.
  • Notifications — the bell tile (and the counter on Home) collects every alert: task assignments and step changes, leave decisions, photo approval results, payslip releases and HR announcements.
Working rhythm that works

A practical cadence for most teams: check the bell first thing in the morning, punch in, work from the Projects list during the day, and punch out at shift end. Because task updates notify both the assignee and the manager instantly, the app effectively replaces the "any update?" follow-up message — status flows to whoever needs it automatically.

Chapter 9
My Profile — Self-Service Rules

My Profile mirrors the full employee record from the web employee module. It is where you keep your working information current — and it is also where the company's data-governance rules are enforced on the phone. Every field falls into one of three tiers, and the app labels the difference clearly so you always know what happens when you save.

You can edit directly

Phone number, personal email, current and permanent address, emergency contacts, blood group and similar contact-level details save immediately. As a safeguard, HR/Admin automatically receive an in-app notification and an email showing the field, the old value and the new value — so every self-service change is transparent to HR without needing approval.

Goes to HR/Admin for approval

Your profile photo is gated. When you upload a new photo it enters a pending state — you see a "submitted" badge, but other colleagues keep seeing the current photo. HR/Admin review it in the Photo Approvals queue and either publish it or reject it with a reason; you are notified of the decision either way.

Locked — contact HR/Admin

Date of birth, first name and last name are identity fields and cannot be edited from self-service on mobile or web — the fields appear disabled. If one of them is wrong (for example a typo from onboarding), raise it with HR/Admin, who will correct the record from the employee module. The same protection applies to every update request you raise from the app.

Sections you will find in My Profile

  • Personal — contact details, addresses, blood group, and your interests chips.
  • Original Birthday — the date used for birthday celebrations; HR/Employee can set it any time, and it may differ from the official date of birth used in records.
  • Employment — department, designation, reporting manager (read-only), plus your technical skills with proficiency level and years of experience.
  • Experience — every previous company with role, duration, the domains you worked in (multiple selection), company logo, category and level, and related letters (offer, relieving, experience, appraisal, payslip) you can attach and open.
  • Achievements — awards and recognitions from your current or previous companies with category, issuer and date.
  • Insurance — your medical insurance details and the family details used for medical cover.
  • Documents & Policies — company documents assigned to you and the policy grid, each with View and Download.
Chapter 10
For HR / Admin — Management Operations

HR/Admin users (and the CEO) carry the organisation's operational load on the same app. The management tiles — Employee Management, Reports and Team Attendance — appear in the Modules grid only for these roles, and their content matches the web modules one-for-one so a task started on web can be finished on mobile and vice versa.

Employee Management

  • Directory & list — search the workforce, view each employee's profile including their total experience computed from their career entries.
  • Add employee — the guided multi-step form (personal, address, employment, bank, emergency) with the same mandatory-field rules as web; required fields are marked so records are complete at creation time.
  • Documents — view an employee's documents with download; identity documents such as Aadhaar can be verified by scanning the QR code from the verification tab.
  • Photo approvals — review employee photo submissions in the queue: approve to publish the new photo, or reject with a mandatory reason; the employee is notified automatically.
  • Help Desk operations — open the Help Desk tile to see all tenant tickets, reply in any thread and change ticket status from the phone; the same queue your employees see under "My tickets" on mobile and on the web Support & AI module.

Reports & Team Attendance

The Reports tile opens leave, attendance, employee and project reports filtered for your permission set — the same reports the web admin dashboard produces, formatted for the phone. Team Attendance gives the day view of who is in, out or on leave with a daily status entry capability for supervisors. A recommended routine: review Team Attendance every morning, clear pending leave approvals before lunch, and skim the Reports module weekly to catch trends — rising regularisations or unused leave — before they become payroll corrections.

Birthday wishes monitoring: the Home birthdays card lists this month's celebrants for the whole month. HR/Admin should keep original birthdays filled in for every employee — celebrations (and the automatic greeting) only fire for employees whose original birthday is recorded. If it is missing, fill it from the employee module on web or mobile.

Chapter 12
Golden Rules, Troubleshooting & Support

A handful of habits keep mobile HRMS usage smooth for everyone. They take seconds to follow and prevent most of the support tickets HR receives — read them once, apply them daily.

Golden rules
  • Punch in and out every working day, with location enabled.
  • Apply for leave in advance; use regularisation only for genuine misses.
  • Keep your profile current — phone, address, emergency contacts, skills, experience letters.
  • Never share your login or install the APK from unofficial sources.
  • Check the notification bell daily; act on approvals the same day.
  • Raise a Help Desk ticket instead of side-channel messages — tickets are tracked, searchable and answered faster.
Escalation path

Step 1 — Employee: try the troubleshooting table first.
Step 2 — HR/Admin: raise the issue with your HR team (in person or via the helpdesk); they can resolve account, data and approval issues.
Step 3 — Super admin: platform-level or technical issues are escalated by HR/Admin to the 3Boxes HRMS super admin with details of the app version and the affected account.

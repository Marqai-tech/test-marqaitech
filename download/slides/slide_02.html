Install the app (one time)
1

Open your company workspace in the phone browser (e.g. marqaitechgroup.3boxeshrms.com) — the download section sits on the login page.

2

Tap the Android app download button — the current release APK (v1.6.6) downloads.

3

Allow 'Install unknown apps' for your browser once, then confirm the install.

4

Upgrades install in place over the old version — permanent signing key, no uninstall needed.
